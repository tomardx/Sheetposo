Sheetposo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Sheetposo is a joke app that shows absurd quotes and sends them as notifications. It works entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Sheetposo has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, because the app never sends any data anywhere.

What is stored on your device

  • The list of quotes you have already been shown, so the app can display your history.
  • The date the app last scheduled its notifications.

Both are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show you quotes during the day.
  • Alarms & reminders — so notifications arrive at the intended times instead of being bunched together by the system.
  • Run at startup — to restore the notification schedule after your phone reboots.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image on your device and hands it to Android's share sheet. What happens next is up to the app you choose. Sheetposo does not upload the image anywhere.

Children

Sheetposo is intended for a general teen and adult audience. It is not directed at children under 13.
